Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to what most homeowners ask before booking their first job with us.

Do you offer free estimates?+

Yes. Every quote starts with a free, no-obligation estimate — either over the phone or with a quick site visit, depending on the scope of work.

Can I bundle multiple services into one job?+

Absolutely — that's the whole idea behind "We Do It All." Most customers combine two or three services, like lawn care with pine straw, or fencing with drainage work, into a single visit.

Are you licensed and insured?+

Yes, Anselmo Pinestraw & Lawnscaping is fully licensed and insured for both residential and commercial work.

Do you work with commercial properties?+

Yes. We service homeowners, HOAs, and commercial properties, including recurring maintenance contracts.

How fast can you schedule my project?+

It depends on the season and scope, but most estimates are returned within one business day, and many smaller jobs can be scheduled within the same week.

What areas do you service?+

We're based in the Midlands region around the 803 area code, covering Columbia, Elgin, Lugoff, Camden, Blythewood, Chapin, Lexington, Sumter and surrounding communities. Call us to confirm your address.
